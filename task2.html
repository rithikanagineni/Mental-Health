Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our mental health services.

How do I know if I need therapy?

If you find yourself struggling with persistent feelings of sadness, anxiety, anger, or overwhelm that affect your daily life, work, relationships, or sleep, therapy can be very helpful. Other signs include difficulty concentrating, loss of interest in activities you once enjoyed, frequent emotional outbursts, or feeling disconnected from yourself and others. You don’t need to be in a crisis to seek support — many people come to therapy for personal growth, stress management, or to build better coping skills. Therapy is a safe space to explore your thoughts and emotions with professional guidance. Remember, asking for help is a sign of strength, not weakness. Our team can help you determine whether therapy is the right step for you during a free consultation.

Are sessions confidential?

Yes, all sessions at MindWellness are completely confidential. Your privacy is our top priority. Everything you share with your therapist remains strictly between you and your counselor, unless there is an immediate risk of harm to yourself or others. We follow strict ethical guidelines and legal requirements to protect your information. Our platform uses secure, encrypted technology for online sessions so your conversations stay private. You can feel safe knowing that your personal story is handled with the utmost care and respect. We are fully compliant with privacy laws and never share your information without your explicit consent.

Do you accept insurance?

We work with most major insurance providers to make mental health care more accessible. During your initial consultation, our team will help verify your insurance benefits and explain your coverage. We also offer flexible self-pay options and sliding scale fees for those without insurance or with high deductibles. Our goal is to remove financial barriers so everyone can receive quality mental health support. If you have any questions about insurance or payment plans, our friendly administrative team is always happy to assist you. Transparency in pricing and coverage is very important to us.

How long does therapy take?

The duration of therapy varies from person to person depending on your goals, the challenges you're facing, and how quickly you respond to treatment. Some people benefit from short-term therapy (6–12 sessions) for specific issues like stress or grief. Others may engage in longer-term therapy (several months to a year) to work through deeper trauma or long-standing patterns. Your therapist will work with you to create a personalized treatment plan and regularly review your progress. You are always in control — you can continue or pause therapy whenever you feel ready. Our focus is on helping you achieve meaningful and lasting change at a pace that feels right for you.

Can I do therapy online?

Yes! We offer secure and convenient online therapy sessions through our encrypted video platform. Online counseling provides the same quality of care as in-person sessions while offering greater flexibility. You can attend sessions from the comfort of your home, office, or anywhere with a stable internet connection. Many clients prefer online therapy because it saves travel time and reduces scheduling stress. Our online platform is user-friendly and fully HIPAA-compliant to ensure your privacy and safety. Whether you choose online or in-person sessions, you will receive the same compassionate and professional support.
